### **Funzy App: Final Smoke Checklist (v5)**

#### **1. First Launch & Authentication**

-   `[ ]` **Onboarding:** Complete the onboarding flow as a new user.
-   `[ ]` **Login/Logout:** Log into an existing account and then log out.
-   `[ ]` **Social Login:** Log in via a social provider (Apple/Google).

#### **2. Feed, Posts & Content**

-   `[ ]` **Feed Loading:** Ensure both Artist and Public feeds load.
-   `[ ]` **Feed Loading:** Check feed's scroll and pagination work as propertly.
-   `[ ]` **Post Creation:** Create and publish a simple text post.
-   `[ ]` **Video Playback:** Play a video, **enter and exit fullscreen mode**.
-   `[ ]` **Audio Playback:** Play an audio track, **background the app, and confirm playback continues**.
-   `[ ]` **Interaction:** Add a reaction and leave a comment on a post.

#### **3. Profile, Subscriptions & Playlist**

-   `[ ]` **Profile:** Open and edit your profile.
-   `[ ]` **Subscriptions (IAP):** Initiate a subscription purchase (until the App Store / Google Play prompt appears).
-   `[ ]` **Playlist Management:** In the playlist section, **reorder two tracks** and save. (NOT IN SCOPE)

#### **4. Gamification & Notifications**

-   `[ ]` **Balance & Perks:** Check 'My Coins' balance and claim a free perk.
-   `[ ]` **Push Notifications:** Receive a push notification and verify that it navigates correctly.
-   `[ ]` **Report Content:** Report a comment.

#### **5. Hardware Integration**

-   `[ ]` **NFC Scan:** Initiate an NFC tag scan.
-   `[ ]` **Music Box Setup:** Initiate a Bluetooth device scan for the Music Box. (NOT IN SCOPE)
-   `[ ]` **Music Box Sync:** Go to Music Box content management and **toggle a track for sync/unsync**. (NOT IN SCOPE)

#### **6. Security & Privacy**

-   `[ ]` **Capture Protection:** Verify screenshot/screen recording is blocked.

---

### **💡 What to Pay Special Attention To (Vulnerable Areas from Bugs):**

-   **When testing the player:** Always test backgrounding/foregrounding the app during playback and exiting fullscreen mode.
-   **When testing Push Notifications:** Ensure that tapping the notification deep-links directly to the correct content, not just the home screen.
-   **When testing IAP:** After simulating a purchase (even if cancelled), return to the app and ensure its state is not 'stuck' waiting for a response from the store.
-   **After all tests:** Relaunch the app (cold start). This is a final check to ensure no actions corrupted the cache or state in a way that would cause a crash on the next launch.
